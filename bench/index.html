Bench · macOS app

Claude Science as a real Mac app.

Bench puts Claude Science in one native window, and adds a small glass panel that shows when a session is working and when it needs you.

Get Bench on GitHub
Requires macOS 27 on Apple silicon, with Claude Science installed
Or install from Terminal
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samporter14/bench/main/install.sh | zsh

What it adds

Lab specimens

While a session works, one of 487 little animated lab specimens plays in a glass panel in the corner of your screen.

A card when it needs you

When a session needs your input, a card takes you straight to it. Other cards tell you when your plan or your context is nearly used up.

One list of what is waiting

The toolbar lists the sessions that need you, the ones still working, and the ones that recently finished.

Context and plan, in the title bar

The title bar shows how much context the open session has used, how much of your plan is left, and when it resets.

A real Mac app

A toolbar, menus, keyboard shortcuts, Settings and Find. A 3 MB download that uses the WebKit already built into macOS.

Keeps to itself

No Bench account, no analytics, no telemetry, no update checks. Bench only talks to Claude Science running on your own Mac.

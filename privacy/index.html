Privacy · October 27, 2026

Privacy, without the specimen jar.

We make software for your work. We don’t want a collection of your work.

A small app is still an app.

Capsid and Nidus keep settings and working data on your Mac. The reviewed code has no Solanum account system or app analytics. That does not make everything you do through them offline: Claude Science, connected services, downloads and this website have their own data flows.

Capsid and Claude Science

Capsid displays Claude Science’s interface and reads local session information so it can show status, questions, plans and results. Claude Science sends your requests through its own service, under that service’s terms and privacy policy. Capsid does not turn Claude Science into an offline model.

Capsid also stores its own settings, Shelf metadata, project working notes and references, and files you choose to save or keep. Kept results live in folders you choose; removing an app does not remove files you saved elsewhere.

Connections with a job to do

  • Updates. Capsid checks GitHub for a new release about once a day by default. Turn this off in Settings → General → Updates. A check sends your IP address and ordinary request information, including the Capsid version in its User-Agent. Check Now and installing an update also contact GitHub.
  • Ask Another Model. This is off by default. If enabled, Capsid contacts the HTTPS provider configured in Settings, including to fetch its model list. Pressing Ask sends your question and any conversation you choose to include to that provider, with your API key. The provider’s terms apply. The key is stored in a file restricted to your Mac account, rather than in Keychain.
  • Phone access. Off by default. When enabled, Capsid serves the phone interface to paired devices through your Tailscale network. Its controls determine whether devices can browse conversations and files or answer, approve and send. You can remove paired devices or turn phone access off.
  • Phone notifications. Optional Web Push notifications use Apple’s push service. Notification contents are encrypted for the receiving device; the push service still handles delivery information. Tailscale and Apple have their own privacy policies.
  • Links and integrations. Opening a website, signing in to a connected service, or running a Shortcut can involve another provider. That provider receives the information needed for the action you request.

Nidus keeps a local notebook.

Nidus stores settings and, depending on your choices and version, session history, projects, goals, inbox items and next-step notes locally. Blocking inspects running apps and browser tabs to apply your rules. Optional Calendar access reads event times to help choose when a session ends; it does not need event titles for this.

Activity recording and document or conversation names are separate options, both off by default in the reviewed current code. If enabled, they can put app and recognized-site durations, plus selected tab or front-window names, into local history and exports. Nidus does not read document contents for Activity. Names can still be private: choose exclusions, leave names off, or use Remove saved names and Clear history in Settings. Disabling a recording option does not erase all previously finished history.

Nidus and Capsid can exchange local focus status. Sharing Capsid task names also needs Capsid’s sharing switch and Nidus’s name option. Optional Plaque sharing writes a local focus snapshot, which can include goal or project text if you choose; sending a page from Plaque is a separate action. Websites or Shortcuts that you ask Nidus to open may make their own network connections.

This website has a host.

These pages use local assets and system fonts. We have not added advertising trackers, forms or analytics scripts to the reviewed site. Cloudflare serves and protects it, so Cloudflare receives connection information such as your IP address, browser request headers and the page requested. Its response headers can also ask browsers to send network error reports. That is data processing, even on a simple static website. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Managing local copies.

Use each app’s settings to control optional recording and sharing. History and saved-name deletion controls affect the app’s local records; exported files, your Mac’s backups and copies you sent elsewhere need to be managed separately. A connected provider keeps information according to its own policy. We do not promise that deleting a local record deletes somebody else’s copy.

Questions, without the research attachment.

Project links lead to GitHub, where issues and comments are public. Please do not post private research, credentials or personal information there. If you contact Sam directly, he receives what you send, including your address, message and attachments. This page will change if these practices change.

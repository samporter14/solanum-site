November 14, 2026 · Capsid 1.1.6

Unpair means unpaired.

  • Unpairing a phone takes effect at once. Remove a device in Settings → Phone, or unpair from the phone itself, and it can't reach your Mac from that moment, even if Capsid couldn't save its list of devices. It stays removed after Capsid restarts, and a photo or message still uploading from that device is stopped before it reaches the session.
  • Quitting won't lose a Working Note. If a note you just typed couldn't be saved, Capsid asks before quitting, as it already did for Shelf legends. Cancel keeps Capsid open with your text still in the panel.
  • Smaller fixes. Ask Another Model no longer marks a new answer Sent when the last one was still being sent. Closing the Shelf stops its search and its thumbnails, so old results don't come back. The Browser window's button now says Open in Browser, because it opens your default browser.

Capsid 1.1.5

Nov 13, 2026
  • Let Claude Science's page sleep. Turn off Settings → General → "Keep Claude Science loaded when its window is closed", and 45 seconds after you close the window Capsid unloads the page and frees its memory. On our test Mac the page's process went from 400 MB (up to 1.3 GB after a day and a half) to under 20 MB. Open the window and your session is back, signed in, in a couple of seconds. Cards for sessions that need you, fail or finish still arrive while it sleeps, and Approve or Reply wakes the page by itself. The switch is on by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it off.
  • Lighter when idle. The Shelf lets go of its search index when you close it, Shelf folders are only re-read when they change, animations reuse frames they already drew, and Capsid checks your sessions less often while nothing is happening.
  • Faster search and reading. Shelf search shows results as it goes, even in large libraries. Quick Open (⌘⇧O) can Search Answers across a project's recent replies, and the reference reader has its own Find (⌘F).
  • Papers and big files. Add several Shelf items to a paper at once, and undo panel changes with ⌘Z. Keep, Results and Save All stream large files straight to disk, up to 512 MB each, with progress, cancel and retry.
  • Easier on the battery. In Low Power Mode, working animations run at 15 fps unless you choose otherwise in Settings → Specimens.
  • Better reading on your phone. Long Markdown documents show in full, a newer draft is no longer cleared by a late answer, the action sheet scrolls in landscape and at large text sizes, Back from a figure returns to where you were, and long table cells can be read.
  • Signing and first launch. Every part of Capsid is signed with its own certificate, innermost parts first, and Install and Reopen checks that certificate before it swaps in an update. If you download the zip in a browser, macOS asks you once to allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The one-line install and Capsid's own Install and Reopen skip that step.

November 6, 2026 · Nidus 0.6.5

Your whole day, not only focus.

  • All-day Activity. Nidus now records which app, website or document was in front, and when, all day. Time stops at your last input after five minutes idle. Days are kept on your Mac for 90 days by default.
  • A two-part menu bar. A smaller Focus starter up top, and Today under it: time at the Mac and in focus, a category split, a mini ribbon and your top three.
  • Four ways to read a day. A day ribbon by category, seven days of bars against the week before, a weekday-by-hour grid, and a click into any app for its sites and files.
  • Drift. What pulled you away: three minutes on something, then three minutes on a distraction straight after.
  • Nidus Today, in widgets and Shortcuts. A widget with today's Work, distracting and other time, and two new actions: Get Today's Activity and How Much Work Today.

Nidus 0.3.0

Oct 6, 2026

Nidus 0.3.0 lets assistants, launchers and Shortcuts drive focus sessions, with real answers.

  • Shortcuts. Start Focus can start a saved setup. New: Add Focus Time, which says how many minutes it actually added. Get Focus Status now says whether a session is running, paused or on a break, whether it's open-ended or strict, and whether it's blocking right now.
  • For scripts and assistants. Run Nidus Command takes one JSON request (status, setups, start, addTime, end) and answers in JSON. A retry with the same request id gets the first answer back instead of doing it twice.
  • Launch links. Right-click a setup and choose Copy Launch Link for a nidus:// link that starts just that setup, by id, so renaming it doesn't break the link.
  • Optional extras in the repository's integrations folder: Raycast Quicklinks, and a small assistant skill (for ChatGPT on your Mac, Codex and similar) that starts a setup, adds time or says how long is left.
  • docs/automation.md covers Shortcuts, links, Raycast, Alfred, BetterTouchTool and the JSON command.

The same rules apply however a session is started: a strict session can't be ended from outside, a start never replaces a session that's on, and asking for status changes nothing. Nidus still never connects to the internet.

Nidus 0.2.0

Oct 5, 2026

Nidus 0.2.0 is the biggest update yet.

  • Setups. Save what you start often, like "Deep work: 90 minutes, only Xcode, strict", and start it in one click from the popover or the right-click menu.
  • Schedules. "Weekdays, 9 to 12" starts a session on its own. A card a minute before lets you skip it or start now.
  • Any length. Type it: "40", "1h30", "until 3:30". Or end a session when your next calendar event starts (Calendar access is asked the first time, and read only on this Mac).
  • Part of a site. Block youtube.com/shorts or reddit.com/r/all and leave the rest.
  • Brave and Chromium join Safari, Chrome and Opera Air.
  • A pause before a snooze. Settings can make Snooze wait 10 or 30 seconds first.
  • Shortcuts and links. Start Focus, End Focus, Toggle Focus and Get Focus Status in Shortcuts, plus nidus://start, end, toggle and popover links. A strict session can't be ended by either.
  • Stats. Export your history as CSV or JSON, a recap card on Mondays, and a calmer Stats page.
  • Quitting puts tabs back. Quit mid-session and blocked tabs return to their pages; they're blocked again when Nidus opens.
  • A new look in places. Settings in toolbar tabs, the popover shows when a session ends and how often something was blocked, and the finish card asks "Did you finish?" first.
